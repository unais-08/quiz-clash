 THE RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

Quick minds
win big.

  1. Gather your crew. Create a room and share the six-character code. 2–4 players per room.
  2. Three rounds, five questions each. Everyone gets the same multiple-choice questions at the same time.
  3. Beat the clock. You have 15 seconds. Correct answer = 100 points. Answer in the first 5 seconds = +50 bonus.
  4. No take-backs. Once an answer is submitted, it’s locked. No answer by timeout? No points.
  5. Highest score wins. Each round has a leaderboard. Top the table after all 15 questions to claim glory.
  6. Stay connected. Refreshing or disconnecting before the match ends forfeits the match for everyone.
